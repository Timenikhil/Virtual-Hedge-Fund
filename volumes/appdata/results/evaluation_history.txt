================================================================================
CONCLUSIONS AND OPEN QUESTIONS
================================================================================

What works:
  - AI allocation adds clear value on homogeneous equity portfolios where
    strategies have high return dispersion and the AI can exploit relative
    Sharpe/momentum differences (Momentum Trio +222pp, Alpha Fund +203pp).
  - The metrics-based context (Run 3 fix) was the single most impactful
    change — going from +51pp to +203pp on Alpha Fund.
  - Infrastructure held: 863 Claude API calls, 0 fallbacks, 0 errors.

What does not work:
  - Cross-asset and alternatives portfolios: AI consistently underperforms
    equal-weight. Root cause: AI lacks macro regime context and overweights
    recent momentum at the expense of long-run diversification value.
  - Factor Blend: near-neutral result (+7pp). When strategy Sharpe ratios
    are clustered (no clear winner), AI signal is too weak to add value.

Open questions for future work:
  1. Would adding a current date / market regime label to the AI context
     help on cross-asset portfolios? (e.g. "current macro regime: rate hike")
  2. Does a longer Sharpe lookback (e.g. 504d / 2yr) outperform 252d?
  3. Would temperature=0 (deterministic Claude responses) reduce weight
     churn and improve weight_stability without hurting returns?
  4. Does prompt-chaining (show Claude prior allocations) reduce churn?
